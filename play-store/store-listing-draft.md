### Full description（≤4000 字符）

```text
Language Repeater is a local video and audio player built for language listening practice.

It plays media files you already have on your device and helps you repeat the exact sentence you want to hear again. The app does not provide, host, stream, or download any movies, TV shows, or other video content. You bring your own local files.

KEY FEATURES

• Sentence-level A/B repeat
Drag the A/B markers on the waveform to loop any segment, or switch to sentence mode to repeat one sentence at a time.

• Waveform and precise seeking
See the audio waveform, sentence boundaries, and A/B markers. Drag the waveform to seek precisely.

• Sentence editing
Split, merge, insert, or delete sentences when automatic segmentation is not perfect.

• Subtitle support
Load your own subtitle files, display bilingual subtitles, and switch subtitle tracks.

• Playback controls
Previous/next sentence, 0.5x–2.0x speed, sleep timer, playlist and history, and resume where you left off.

• Background playback
Keep listening with the screen off. Control playback from the notification, lock screen, or headset/Bluetooth buttons.

• Media library
Browse local videos and audio, filter by folder, and add multiple items to your playlist.

• Gestures
Tap to pause/play, double-tap to return to the start of the current sentence, swipe left/right to change sentences, long-press for speed, and slide on the left/right side to adjust brightness or volume.

PRIVACY

Media files are processed on your device. The app does not upload your videos, audio, or subtitle files to the developer or to Firebase. The app uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for usage statistics and crash diagnostics; see the privacy policy for details.

The app has no account system and no ads.
```

### 完整说明（≤4000 字符）

```text
复读机（Language Repeater）是一款为语言精听设计的本地音视频播放器。

它播放你设备上已有的音视频文件，帮助你反复听同一句话。应用不提供、不托管、不下载、不串流任何电影、电视剧或其他影视内容；你需要自己准备本地文件。

主要功能

• 句子级 A/B 复读
在波形上拖动 A/B 标记即可循环任意片段；也可以切换到句子模式，逐句重复。

• 波形与精确定位
显示音频波形、句子分隔线和 A/B 标记，拖动波形即可精确跳转。

• 句子编辑
自动分句不理想时，可以分割、合并、插入或删除句子。

• 字幕支持
导入自己的字幕文件，显示双语字幕，切换字幕轨道。

• 播放控制
上一句/下一句、0.5x–2.0x 倍速、定时关闭、播放列表和历史记录，并自动恢复上次播放位置。

• 后台播放
熄屏后继续听；可以从通知栏、锁屏、耳机或蓝牙按键控制播放。

• 媒体库
浏览本地视频和音频，按文件夹筛选，多选加入播放列表。

• 手势操作
单击暂停/播放，双击回到当前句开头，左右滑动切换上下句，长按倍速，左侧上下滑调亮度，右侧上下滑调音量。

隐私说明

音视频和字幕文件在设备本地处理，应用不会把媒体文件上传给开发者或 Firebase。应用使用 Firebase Analytics 和 Crashlytics 进行使用统计和崩溃诊断，详情见隐私政策。

应用无账号体系、无广告。
```
